# Intent - Assignment 1

> Supplemental agentic-workflow document added for organization; not a professor-required file.

## Goal

Create a transparent, reproducible, and easy-to-explain Assignment 1 submission that demonstrates how an AI assistant supported an end-to-end data science workflow without replacing student judgment.

## Required outcomes

- Part 1 presents the House Prices project from problem definition through interpretation.
- Part 2 presents three selected replications of professor examples: customer segmentation, market basket analysis, and anomaly detection.
- GitHub contains the notebooks, scripts, data or data-access instructions, prompts/chat evidence, figures, results, reports, and video links used in the submission.
- The student can explain why each step was performed and paraphrase each result in plain language.
- Missing evidence is labeled as TODO rather than reconstructed from memory and presented as fact.

## Constraints

- Keep the legacy `Assignment1/` folder unchanged until the new structure is fully verified.
- Use "Assignment 1 - Part 2" consistently for the replication work.
- Do not claim that a notebook was executed when its saved execution counts and outputs are empty.
- Do not treat approximate or legacy metrics as newly reproduced results.
- Do not include Medium publication as a deliverable.

## Success criteria

The assignment is ready only when all critical items in `TODO.md` are complete, the repository and videos are accessible, every quantitative claim is traceable to an executed artifact, and the student can narrate the end-to-end journey without reading AI output verbatim.
